ACADEMY RULES

General Rules

1. Maintain Silence and Discipline in Class
Students must maintain silence and discipline in the classroom. When the teacher is conducting the class, students must not discuss or talk with anyone unnecessarily.

2. ID Card
Bringing and wearing the Academy ID Card is compulsory.

3. Absence Without Prior Information
If a student remains absent without informing the Director (Sir), a fine of ₹20 per day will be charged. If a student requires leave for more than 2 days, they must submit a written leave application to the Director (Sir).

4. Absence for More Than 5 Days
If a student remains absent for more than 5 days without informing the Director, their registration will become invalid. If they wish to continue attending classes, they will have to complete the re-registration process.

5. Punctuality and Late Arrival
Students must arrive 5 minutes before their scheduled class time. A fine of ₹10 will be charged for arriving more than 5 minutes after the class has started. No excuse for late arrival will be accepted. Students should inform the Director (Sir) in advance if they expect to be late.

6. Office Rules
Students are strictly prohibited from sitting in the Academy Office without the permission of the Director (Sir).

7. Bringing Friends to Class/Office
Students must not bring friends along to the classroom or office. Violation of this rule may result in disciplinary action.

8. Respectful Behaviour Towards Teachers
Students must maintain good manners and respectful behaviour towards their teachers.

9. Discipline and Respect Among Classmates
Students must maintain discipline in class and behave respectfully with their classmates. All students must treat one another with dignity and respect.

10. Electronic Devices
Students are not permitted to take or use electronic devices such as mobile phones, laptops, etc. in the classroom. Students must deposit their mobile phones with their teacher upon entering the class.

11. Misbehaviour
If a student misbehaves with a teacher or the Director, they may be removed from the Academy.

12. Complaints and Problems
If a student faces any problem or has any complaint, they should report it to the Academy Director.
Director: Pankaj Kumar Azad

13. Photography and Videography
No student may take photographs or videos without the permission of the teacher. Students must follow the Academy's instructions regarding photography and videography.

14. Exchange of Mobile Numbers
Students must not exchange mobile numbers with classmates without informing the Director (Sir).

15. Comments on Classmates
Students must not make inappropriate comments about their classmates. Violation of this rule may result in disciplinary action.

16. Leaving the Academy After Class
Students must leave the Academy within 5 minutes after the class ends.

17. Leaving the Academy Together
Male and female students must not leave the Academy premises together.

18. Permission During Class
If a student needs to do anything during class, they must first take permission from the Director/teacher. This includes drinking water, attending to an urgent matter, or dealing with any other problem.

19. Use of Academy Property
Students must not use any Academy property without the permission of the Director (Pankaj Sir). Examples: Sound Box, Paper, Guitar, etc.

Monthly Fee Rules

20. Monthly Fee Payment
The monthly fee must be deposited between the 1st and 10th of every month. If the fee is not paid by the 10th, a late fee of ₹50 for every 5 days will be charged.

21. Difficulty in Paying Monthly Fees
If a student faces any difficulty in paying the monthly fee, they must submit an application to the Director (Pankaj Sir) stating the appropriate reason. The request will be valid only after approval by the Director.

22. Continuous Absence
If a student remains absent continuously for 3 months, they will have to complete the re-registration process.

Dance Class Rules

23. Dance Dress Code
Students must not enter the classroom without the prescribed dance dress/uniform. Students must change into their class clothes before the class starts.

Dance Class Structure

  • Total Class Duration: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • 30 minutes: Workout / Exercise
  • 30 minutes: Basic Training
  • 30 minutes: Song Practice / Dance

Dance Class Timings

  • Morning: 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM
  • Evening: 3:00 PM – 8:00 PM

24. Dance Practice
Students must not practise dance to any song other than the song instructed/approved by the Director (Sir).

Disciplinary Rule

25. Violation of Rules
If a student is found violating any of the above rules, they will be subject to disciplinary action.

Singer Class

26. Singer Teacher Class Timings

  • Sunday: 3:30 PM – 5:30 PM
  • Thursday: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Practice Timing: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM
Singing Class Closure: Sunday to Monday (Wednesday) — as stated in the original schedule.

Guitar Class

27. Guitar Class Timings

  • Sunday: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM
  • Wednesday: 1:30 PM – 3:30 PM

Important

All students are expected to follow the Academy Rules, maintain discipline, and respect the Director, teachers, staff, and fellow students.
